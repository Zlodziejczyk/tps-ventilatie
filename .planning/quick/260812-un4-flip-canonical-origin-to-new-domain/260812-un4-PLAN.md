# Quick Task 260812-un4: Flip CANONICAL_ORIGIN to the rebranded domain

## Why now

`tpsklimaattechniek.nl` went live on 2026-08-12 after the dd24 (Key-Systems)
contact-verification suspension was lifted. Verified from outside:

- `https://www.tpsklimaattechniek.nl/` → 200, valid cert, `server: Vercel`
- `https://tpsklimaattechniek.nl/` → 308 → www
- apex `A` → `216.198.79.1`; `www` `CNAME` → `863afab58c3e6cf4.vercel-dns-017.com`

So **www is the Vercel Production host** and the apex redirects to it. The codebase
still points every absolute URL at the retired brand `https://tpsventilatie.nl`.

The pre-existing "canonical origin is the apex" convention (ROADMAP.md,
assert-seo messages) is superseded by the www choice — comments and assertion
messages are updated to match rather than left lying.
