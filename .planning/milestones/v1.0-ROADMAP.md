# Milestone v1.0: Launch

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-08-12
**Phases:** 1–7
**Total Plans:** 53

## Overview

Expanded the TPS site from a 6-page pre-launch proposal into a full ~22-page, SEO-driven, lead-generation climate-tech site (Airconditioning, Warmtepompen, WTW, Mechanische Ventilatie) and took it to launch-ready. The build was strictly dependency-ordered: a single typed taxonomy model fed route generation, navigation, sitemap, and JSON-LD; a data-driven template rendered the 20+ pages; SEO infrastructure instrumented those pages before any copy landed; unique owner-reviewed Dutch content filled them (the real quality gate against the Dec-2025 Core Update); the lead path was then secured and hardened — resolving the static-export-vs-hybrid decision that gated form security, instant owner notification, and image optimization; and finally the home page was rebuilt for conversion and the whole site brought to WCAG 2.1 AA. Every workstream served one outcome: turn local search demand into contacted leads with the owner notified instantly.
