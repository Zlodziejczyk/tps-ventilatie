| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-9-legacy-token | Denial of service (of verification) | legacy TXT record | mitigate | D-07 rule in README/runbook AND asserted by `verify-measurement.ts` check 1 on every run |
| T-9-owner-delegation | Elevation | GSC Owner role | mitigate | Owner granted only to the confirmed client identity; a rejected/unknown address is recorded, never guessed |
| T-9-vercel-token | Information disclosure | `VERCEL_TOKEN` | mitigate | optional; shortest expiry; shell env for one run; never in `.env.example` values, git, or Actions |

| T-9-SC | Tampering | npm installs | accept (n/a) | none |
