<objective>
Landing 2 of 4 (D-09/D-10) — the riskiest single edit in the phase, which is exactly why it gets its
own diff and its own revert. Unify the indexability predicate to `status === "published"` for every
node type (D-20) and relabel the 6 statics so their `status` finally tells the truth (IDX-05). The
sitemap does not move: 5 indexable before, 5 indexable after. Same pages, honest data, one lever.

**Blocking prerequisite discovered in research (08-RESEARCH.md §Pitfall 2).** D-20 as written cannot
build. `pageSchema` applies the anti-thin-content bar to EVERY `review|published` node, so flipping
the 5 content statics to `published` yields **13 Zod violations** — verified: home/projecten/over-ons/
contact have empty shells, and `tarieven` fails on `steps` despite its 122-word intro. Statics render
bespoke hand-built pages; their taxonomy `content` shell is metadata plus optional SEO prose. The bar
exists to stop thin *taxonomy-rendered* pages, so it must be scoped to the types whose content is
actually rendered from the shell. Authoring 120 words + steps + FAQs into five nodes that render none
of it is precisely the "data says one thing, the page does another" class D-11 rejects.

Purpose: make indexability purely data — one predicate, no exceptions — without weakening the content
gate where it earns its keep.
Output: a scoped content bar, a one-line predicate, six honest `status` values, and a named safety
belt for the legal page. Green at 5 indexable pages.
</objective>
