the `/diensten` hub, render every word of it on the page, publish the node, and introduce
`INDEXABLE_FLOOR = 27` in the same commit that makes 27 true. Sitemap 26 → 27, `noindex` gone from the

precisely why IDX-03 exists — and why 08-02's "published hub with an empty shell must be REJECTED"
assertion is the guard that forces this plan to do real work before the flip.

**Two rendering traps, both resolved in research (08-RESEARCH.md §Pattern 4, §Open Questions).**

pillar pages `ServiceHero` displays it. `/diensten` has a hardcoded hero and does not use
`ServiceHero`, so naive wiring would make the authored intro's first sentence render nowhere. And
`ServiceSteps` is currently imported only by the sub-service route — the 4 pillar pages each carry 4
authored steps that render nowhere at all. The hub must not join them.

Purpose: give the umbrella page a reason to rank, and make "complete" a single named number that the
build gate and the live probe cannot disagree about.

a preview at 27 URLs.
