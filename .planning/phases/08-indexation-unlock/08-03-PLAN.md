<objective>
Landing 3 of 4 (D-09) — **the unlock**. Flip all 21 `review` service nodes to `published`, taking the
site from 5 indexable pages to 26. This is the requirement the whole milestone stacks behind: 21
finished, owner-approved pages that Google has never been allowed to index.

The flip itself is trivial by design — the two landings before it did the work. `pageSchema` has
applied the ≥120-word / ≥1-step / 3–6-FAQ bar to `review` nodes since Phase 4, so all 21 already
clear it (verified: a full-flip clone validates). 08-01 replaced the gate that hardcoded
`entries.length === 5` and would otherwise have blocked its own fix. 08-02 made `status` the single
lever. What remains is a data edit plus the structural assertions that keep it honest.

Purpose: turn on the service surface, and make "a pillar went dark" a build failure rather than a
discovery months later.
Output: 21 `published` nodes, named per-pillar and per-sub-service indexability assertions, and a
preview serving 26 URLs with no `noindex` on any of them.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| owner approval → published status | The status-flip commit IS the editorial approval record; publishing content the owner has not approved is a reputational and YMYL risk |
| taxonomy data → crawler-visible output | 21 pages become crawlable in one commit; a content defect that survived `review` now reaches Google |
| gate coverage → partial collapse | A subset of pages silently reverting to non-indexable is invisible to a bare count |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-8-dark | Repudiation | One pillar or sub-service silently non-indexable | mitigate (this plan) | Named per-pillar and per-sub-service indexability assertions with a `>=17` floor; negative proof that a reverted node fails the build |
| T-8-thin | Tampering | A thin page reaching the index at publish | mitigate | `pageSchema` applies the same bar at `review` and `published`, so all 21 cleared it in Phase 4; `validate-taxonomy` runs on every build from 08-01 |
| T-8-claim | Information Disclosure | A forbidden YMYL claim going public | mitigate | `assert-no-forbidden-claims.ts` already scans `review` nodes and is build-blocking from 08-01; the flip changes nothing it scans |
| T-8-scope | Tampering | Copy/metadata edits smuggled into the flip commit | mitigate (this plan) | Diff assertion: only `status:` lines may change in `lib/services/` |
| T-8-approval | Repudiation | No durable record of who approved publication | mitigate (this plan) | Commit message names the 21 pages and cites the 2026-08-05 owner sign-off (P4 D-08) |
</threat_model>
