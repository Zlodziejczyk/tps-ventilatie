session; within minutes Claude confirms the zone at all three nameservers, runs the full one-hop probe

live CI gate.

verification here is sized so that a failure is *seen* inside the window where reverting two records
still restores the prior state. After roughly 3 to 4 weeks, Google has consolidated and a revert becomes
a second migration.
Output: a repointed zone, a green live probe, valid certificates, committed mail headers, and a declared
cutover date.
