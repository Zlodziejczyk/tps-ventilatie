<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| registrar panel → the live zone | the only external system this phase mutates; it carries MX, SPF, DMARC and DKIM for the owner's working mailbox |
| dd24 nameservers → resolvers worldwide | a change is not a change until all three agree, and cached copies persist for their old TTL regardless |
| the SPF record → who may send as this domain | widening or mistiming it hands send authority to whoever owns the apex address |
| the GSC TXT record → three Search Console properties | not a secret, but behaves like one: removing it silently unverifies all three and kills the Change of Address |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-spf-widen | Spoofing | the SPF record during the cutover window | mitigate | `a` is dropped at least eight hours before the apex moves, verified at all three nameservers, with the earliest-flip timestamp recorded and re-checked by the next plan |
| T-10-gsc-ownership | Tampering | the `google-site-verification` TXT | mitigate | named as must-not-touch on the edit sheet and asserted present at all three nameservers immediately after the session; its loss is a stop-everything condition |
| T-10-mail-records | DoS | MX, the `mail` A record, DMARC, DKIM | mitigate | explicitly out of scope on the edit sheet and asserted unchanged at all three nameservers; the zone diff must contain nothing beyond the two TTLs and the SPF string |
| T-10-partial-push | Repudiation | zone propagation | mitigate | all three authoritative nameservers, read twice about five minutes apart; a disagreement is treated as mid-push and waited out, never re-edited |
| T-10-registrar-creds | Elevation | dd24 access | mitigate | no automation, no stored registrar credential, no Claude-driven browser in that panel (D-07); the user performs every edit |
